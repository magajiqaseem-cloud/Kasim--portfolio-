360° viewer test bench

This page proves the 360° viewer that is built into the Aureon and Palmstone demos. The object below is a neutral test model made for checking the controls, not a car or a property. Try it on a phone: swipe to turn it, leave it alone and it turns slowly by itself.

What the demos need to switch their 360° buttons on

  • Car (Aureon GT Coupé): one GLB model of that one car, or 36 to 72 photos/renders taken at equal steps all the way round it, same light, same background.
  • Estate (Palmstone waterfront villa): one GLB model of that one building and its grounds, or a full set of matching exterior frames around it.
  • Add the files under demos/viewer360/assets/ and an entry in assets.json. The buttons appear by themselves.
