    How a shipment works with us.

    Four steps, one contact person, and an update at every stage.

    1. Request a quote

      Tell us what, where and when. You get a fixed price within 24 hours.

    2. Book and collect

      We reserve space with the carrier and collect your cargo from your door.

    3. Customs and loading

      Our team files the documents and clears customs before departure.

    4. Delivery

      Track it live, and we confirm with photos when it arrives.
