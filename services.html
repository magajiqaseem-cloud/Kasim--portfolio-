How a project runs

Four stages, no surprises.

Every engagement follows the same path, whether it is a single video edit or a full website.

  1. 1

    Discovery call

    We agree on your goals, audience, market and what a successful result looks like.

  2. 2

    Scope & plan

    You get a written plan: deliverables, milestones, timeline and price, before work starts.

  3. 3

    Design & build

    Work happens in reviewable stages, with updates at every milestone.

  4. 4

    Launch & support

    Final files and deployment, plus a handover so you can run it with confidence.
